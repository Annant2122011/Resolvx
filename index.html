THE QUERYFIXR PRINCIPLE

Support should feel like a process, not a scavenger hunt.

When help requests live in scattered channels, the details vanish, ownership gets fuzzy, and the same questions come back around. QueryFixr is designed to give every request a home, every staff action a clear path, and every important change a traceable record.
